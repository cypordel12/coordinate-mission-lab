THE OPERATION

The UAV performs an agricultural spraying mission. When its battery becomes low, it requests a rendezvous with a mobile UGV carrying one compatible, fully charged spare battery. COORDINATE compares approved service bays and explains the operational trade-offs to the farm manager.

02 DECISION FRAMEWORK

Three ways to
coordinate.

The revised model compares the strategies using the same field, resources, constraints and scenario inputs.
01 BASELINE

Static method

Uses the central approved service bay and nominal UAV speed. It does not adapt the meeting choice to wind, obstacle or resource conditions.

Fixed reference↗
02 GREEDY

Dynamic heuristic

At nominal speed, selects the feasible candidate with the shortest combined UGV and UAV geometric travel distance. It does not optimize waiting cost globally.

Distance-first↗
03 PROPOSED DSS
✳

Proposed optimization

Plans vehicle-specific routes, searches a continuous UAV cruise speed for each candidate, removes infeasible options, and ranks the remaining alternatives by the normalized compromise score.

Model defined · API pending↗

04 PROJECT BOUNDARIES

Built to recommend.
Not to take control.

COORDINATE is a simulation-based decision support prototype. It recommends and explains a feasible stationary battery-swap rendezvous; the farm operations manager retains final decision authority.
